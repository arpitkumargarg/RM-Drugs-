Our Story

R.M. Drugs is an established Indian pharmaceutical company engaged in the marketing, distribution, and export of generic medicines, nutraceuticals, and ayurvedic formulations to markets across India and the globe.

Built over four and a half decades, our distribution network spans pharmacies, government and private hospitals, and clinics nationwide and extends to partners across international markets. A team of over 100 dedicated professionals, spanning management, quality assurance, and export operations, drives a business built on trust, consistency, and a genuine commitment to healthcare.

Every product we supply comes from WHO-GMP certified manufacturing facilities across India, ensuring consistent quality, safety, and regulatory compliance in every market we serve.

Our Legacy

Built on Decades of Trust

Four and a half decades in India's pharmaceutical trade have taught us one thing: reliable healthcare access is built on relationships, not just transactions. R.M. Drugs was founded on that principle, and it still shapes how we work today.

Over the years, we've built deep expertise in procurement, identifying the right GMP certified manufacturing partners, vetting them rigorously, and ensuring every product that carries our name meets the standards our partners and patients deserve. This isn't a skill you build overnight. It comes from decades of relationships across India's manufacturing landscape, and an instinct for quality that only experience can teach.

Behind every order is a team of over 100 professionals who bring this experience to the table daily in sourcing, quality assurance, regulatory affairs, and supply chain operations. It's this depth of expertise, built patiently over generations, that lets our partners trust us with something as important as healthcare.

Our legacy isn't just years in business. It's the knowledge, relationships, and reliability that come with them.

About AIDE

Our international export brand

We hold exclusive rights for distribution, marketing, and export of pharmaceutical, nutraceutical and ayurvedic products for the AIDE brand, a registered trademark, from India to the global market.

For our partners, AIDE means more than a product on the shelf. It's a brand backed by consistent quality and 45 years of trust, offering stronger shelf appeal and better margins than unbranded generics.

Thousands of our customers spanning the globe can vouch for the better efficacy and strong results of AIDE products.

Our Mission

To deliver high-quality and affordable pharmaceutical products to patients and healthcare systems across the globe bridging the gap between world-class pharmaceutical quality and competitive pricing, without compromising on safety, efficacy, or compliance.
